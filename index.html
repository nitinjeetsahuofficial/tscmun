About TSCMUN

The Sovereign Council Model United Nations

The Sovereign Council Model United Nations (SCMUN) is an independent Model United Nations initiative established in 2026 with the vision of fostering diplomatic awareness, critical thinking, leadership and international cooperation among young individuals.

SCMUN seeks to create an intellectually stimulating platform where delegates engage in structured debate, represent diverse national perspectives and deliberate upon pressing global challenges.

Through diplomacy, dialogue and collaborative problem-solving, SCMUN aims to empower the next generation of leaders and responsible global citizens.
